### Changed

- **The Evidence tab opens on the evidence bundle, the report shares the console's look, and the dashboard, the submit dialog and the analyses list are simpler (T-466).** **Evidence** now leads with the evidence bundle, read from its JSON: a sections navigator (a picker on a phone), one section at a time with its Markdown read by markdown-it as the report's own renderer reads it and drawn as React elements, never as HTML (a tag in the report stays text, a link is made only to a place in the bundle or an http, https or mailto address, an image is its alt text, and a section too long to lay out, or one whose drawing fails, is shown as its raw text), the evidence index with a search, an agent and a result filter, the notes that cite each row and a link to the row's full output, and the notes the analyst report cites with each evidence id linked to its row. The bundle's anchors are the bundle HTML's own, so `#ev_0007`, `#cite-3` and `#sec-…` (a section, or the subsection it opens on) land where they land in the downloaded file. Every tool call's arguments and output are under **Records**, the tab's second view, with the sections no tab draws; a record link written before (`?evidence=ev_0007`) still opens its record there. Run quality stays one folded section at the top of the tab, and before a run has a report the records lead as they did. The **analyst report and evidence bundle HTML** (and so the PDF) print in the console's light tokens, type scale and five-rung severity colours, with tables drawn as the console draws them and a cover that leads with the verdict word in its colour, its confidence, the severity and the family; the analyst report opens with its cover and executive summary before the contents, and the Overview tab follows the same order (key findings, the summary, then the questions asked of the run). The single-file layout prints byte for byte as before, and the document policy and style nonce are unchanged. The **dashboard** shows recent analyses and the verdict mix; operator alerts (test mode, a missing enrichment worker, unavailable tools) are shown to administrators only and in plain words, with a link to the setting instead of its key; every other reader is told in one line when test mode is on and results may be simulated, or when that is not known. **Submitting** is one drop zone: the uploaded file opens its dialog with "Start analysis" focused, and the providers, profile, questions and an attached sandbox report are under a collapsed "Advanced" section that says how many choices differ from the defaults. The **analyses list** has a search and a row of status filters, and each row has one actions button beside its link (copy SHA-256, copy job id, and cancel while the run is going). **Settings** uses the console's tab row, buttons and cards; its pages do what they did.
