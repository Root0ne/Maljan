- **A negation after a parent technique's rarest own word that denies another object no longer keeps the word from affirming (T-531).** "Not packed; strings are obfuscated so that none show in plain text." and "Not packed; the obfuscation leaves no imports." were asked as the absence of Obfuscated Files or Information, since any negating word later in the clause was read against "obfuscated". A "so" that opens a result with its own subject ("so that", "so the", "so no") now ends the clause, and a "no" that writes the verb's own object right after the word ("leaves no imports") denies that object. After a form of "be" or "have" ("obfuscation is no concern", "has no effect"), before a noun of absence ("shows no sign", "no longer"), after a colon ("obfuscated strings: no hits") or a "so" before an adjective ("is so unlikely"), the negation still denies the word.
