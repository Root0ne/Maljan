### Fixed

- **A negation after a parent technique's rarest own word that denies another object no longer keeps the word from affirming (T-531).** "Not packed; strings are obfuscated so that none show in plain text." and "Not packed; the obfuscation leaves no imports." were asked as the absence of Obfuscated Files or Information, since any negating word later in the clause was read against "obfuscated". A "so" that opens a result with a subject of its own ("so that", "so the", "so no") now ends the clause, unless its negation stands with a word of presence ("so the analysis found none", "so that none was found"); a "so" before a pronoun ("so it is not present") or an adjective ("so unlikely") ends nothing. A "no" right after the word denies the object of an effect verb only ("leaves", "removes", "strips", "hides", "conceals", "keeps" and their forms), never a noun of absence ("leaves no trace"). After any other verb ("plays no role", "returned no hits", "is no concern") or a noun ("scan no hits"), the negation still denies the word. No stored benchmark claim is asked differently.
