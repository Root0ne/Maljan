### Fixed

- **A negation after a parent technique's rarest own word that denies another object no longer keeps the word from affirming (T-531).** "Not packed; strings are obfuscated so that none show in plain text." and "Not packed; the obfuscation leaves no imports." were asked as the absence of Obfuscated Files or Information, since any negating word later in the clause was read against "obfuscated". A "so" that opens a result with a subject of its own ("so that", "so the", "so no") now ends the clause ("so the strings are not visible"), unless its negation stands with a word of presence ("so the analysis found none", "so no obfuscation was identified", "so the result was no") or with the word's own stem again ("so the sample is not obfuscated"); a "so" before a pronoun ("so it is not present") or an adjective ("so unlikely") ends nothing. A "no" right after the word denies only what the word leaves or keeps of the sample: after "leave" or "keep" and before imports, strings, readable or plain(-text) names, cleartext values, API names or exports ("leaves no names resolvable", "keeps no strings in clear text"), and only when nothing but a closed set of words ("readable", "visible", "resolvable", "observable", "intact", "in clear text", "at all", "calls") follows the object in its clause, so "leaves no strings obfuscated" and "keeps no imports concealed" are still asked. After any other verb ("hides no payload", "plays no role", "returned no hits"), before any other object ("leaves no trace", "left no residue") or after a noun ("scan no hits"), the negation still denies the word. No stored benchmark claim is asked differently.
