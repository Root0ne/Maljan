### Fixed

- **The Markdown, the HTML and the PDF draw every part of a section, every cell under a heading, tool text as written and no cut inside a defang mark (T-342, T-343, T-347, T-348).** A section keeps the kind of the first answer that opened it, and the Markdown body drew only the part that kind names: a tool that answered once in prose and once with records lost its rows on every page. A section now draws its rows, its items and its text whatever its kind; a tool's own text keeps its fence, with the rows after it, and the text beside a table is still the prose note above it. A table merged from analysts' listings of other widths carried cells past its columns, and the Markdown cut every row to its columns, so the HTML and the PDF dropped them: the report writer now gives the table the union of its columns (a later artifact's cell under the column of its own heading, added when the table has none and the heading reads the table the same; the payload, and a cell with no heading of its own, at its position, headed `Payload` where that is where a reader takes it from, `Column N` otherwise; a row whose named layout would read another type, value or payload is laid out by position), and a stored table wider than its columns is drawn with the extra cells headed by position (`Column 4`), as the console heads them. The Markdown escaped every character that makes Markdown of a tool's text but `_`, so a banner such as `[____DOWNLOADING____]` or a name such as `__init__` came out bold in the HTML and the PDF: an underscore that can open or close emphasis is now escaped, after the defanger has read the value (so `_ldap._tcp.example.com` is still defanged as a host), and one inside a word (`FUN_00401310`) is written as it is. A cell is defanged before it is cut, and a cut could fall inside the mark (`skinnyjeanso[.…`): it now falls before the mark. The surface checker reads the Markdown and the console's section body as they now draw: it looks for each cell, item and text under the section's own heading (the i-th voiced heading of its title, where the body shares the title), checks that a section with rows draws a table with a heading for every cell of its widest row and a row for each of its distinct rows, and lists a section it found no heading for under `not_checked` instead of searching the page silently. On the stored runs only the rows, cells and emphasis named here change on the page; the Markdown source also writes `\_` for such underscores.
