### Fixed

- **A field value leaves out the emphasis its label closes after the colon (T-550).** A label marked colon and all, `**EVIDENCE:** [ev_0002]`, kept the closing `**` at the head of its value, so the evidence was stored and shown as `** [ev_0002]`, an unread CONFIDENCE was quoted as `** high` and a TECHNIQUE line asked about began with `** `. A run of `*` or `_` right after an EVIDENCE, CONFIDENCE or TECHNIQUE label's colon with whitespace or the line's end after it is now no part of the value, at a line start and after whitespace alike; emphasis that opens the value (`EVIDENCE: **[ev_0002]**`) stays in it. A field that holds nothing but that emphasis is an empty field: an evidence field of only `**` is a claim without evidence (or with the ids its sentence cites), as `EVIDENCE:` written with nothing after it is, rather than one citing `**`. Over 1,000,000 generated texts every read that changes holds this spelling and either equals the old read with the closing emphasis taken off each value, or reads such an empty field as empty. One stored model text of the benchmark runs holds the spelling in claim blocks it reads: its 19 claims now cite their evidence without the leading `** `, and nothing else in the read changes.
