### Fixed

- **A validation retry after a revision that wrote no claim no longer replaces the claims in force (T-335).** A revision with no claim line made no decision about the analyst's answer in force, but the revision node read that from the answer the validation turn left. A retry the turn asked for, a turn with no tools, had by then written claims of its own, and those replaced every claim in force, including the ones the analyst had kept when asked. In one run the reverser's 98 claims were replaced by the retry's 37. The revision path now records, before the validation turn, that the revision began no claim block at all (a block the parser could not read, for want of a readable confidence, is a claim the revision wrote), and the node reads that, for every analyst. The claims in force stand. The retry's claims are added beside them, each marked with the round whose retry added it (`added_by_retry_in_round`); a claim that states one in force again in substance (its values, cited entries and technique) is not added twice, and the count left out is logged. The retry's new findings, artifacts, open questions and disputes are added after the ones in force, the added claims follow the answer in force in the reports the next round and the mediator read, and the round's record counts them per analyst (`retry_added`). The mark reads "added by a tool-less retry, round N" in the judge's summary of the answers, the composer's claim lines, the report's list of claims the body leaves out, the transcript event and the web transcript and analyst findings. Corroboration and the ATT&CK table, which the STIX export reads, do not count such a claim as support for its technique, or its analyst as an independent layer naming it, unless it cites an entry its own analyst's tool calls filed; a technique only such claims name is published with the note, and so is its attack-pattern in a bundle built because the judge's answer was not one. A revision that wrote claims of its own still replaces the answer in force, a retry still merges into that revision as before, and a claim is still taken out only by a `WITHDRAW` line.
