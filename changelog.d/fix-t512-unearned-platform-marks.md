### Security

- **A sandbox's own report never sets the platform's marks (T-512).** A provider's document could carry a top-level `synthetic` or `recorded_fixture` key, and the readers passed it on: a live detonation, or an attached report, then read as the mock sandbox's empty stand-in (no sandbox ran, its observations withheld from the analysts) or as a recorded fixture, and kept no target claim to compare with the sample. At the intake of every report a provider hands the run, live and attached alike, each platform mark the report's reader does not set itself (`ReportReader.platform_marks`) is taken out of the report's fields, its published parts and its kept blocks, and a warning names it; the run reads the report as the sandbox's observation. Only the mock sandbox's reader sets the marks, so its stand-in and its recorded fixtures read as before, and a report that carries no mark is left byte for byte as it came. Only the built-in mock reader instance earns its marks (`readers.earned_marks`, by identity): a plug-in reader that declares marks, or one registered in the mock's place, earns none. No operator can name the mock's reader for a sandbox's reports: `sandbox.rest.report.format`, a declared report format's `reader` (refused at save), a format accepted for an upload and an upload read by such a format are refused in words (`readers.named_reader_refusal`). The marks are one list (`sandbox_document.PLATFORM_MARKS`), and `pipeline.sandbox_status` imports its keys from it. The run's mark for a report naming another target than the sample (`target_not_the_sample`, T-322) is in that list and defined beside the others: no reader earns it, so a sandbox's own key under that word is taken out at intake with a warning, and the run's removal of it before setting its own mark stays as a second guard. A stored configuration that already names the mock's reader (`sandbox.rest.report.format`, or a declared format's `reader`) now blocks every settings save until it is changed; the refusal names the key to change.
