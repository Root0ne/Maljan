### Security

- **A sandbox's own report never sets the platform's marks (T-512).** A provider's document could carry a top-level `synthetic` or `recorded_fixture` key, and the readers passed it on: a live detonation, or an attached report, then read as the mock sandbox's empty stand-in (no sandbox ran, its observations withheld from the analysts) or as a recorded fixture, and kept no target claim to compare with the sample. At the intake of every report a provider hands the run, live and attached alike, each platform mark the report's reader does not set itself (`ReportReader.platform_marks`) is taken out of the report's fields, its published parts and its kept blocks, and a warning names it; the run reads the report as the sandbox's observation. Only the mock sandbox's reader sets the marks, so its stand-in and its recorded fixtures read as before, and a report that carries no mark is left byte for byte as it came. The marks are one list (`sandbox_document.PLATFORM_MARKS`) a later platform mark joins.
