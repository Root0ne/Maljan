A rewrite that says a call failed is put in only where, for each redone call it cites, rests on or names, it cites a call that redid it and nowhere says the call was never redone (a negated redo word, "for good", "permanently", "never succeeded") (`agreement.failure_redone`); a rewrite names a call more broadly than a sentence is held for (any "listing" or "lister" names every call that lists code, as a refused rewrite only keeps the sentence as written); its words for a redo never pass it alone, and the model is told why and asked once more. The rewrite is read clause by clause (at each semicolon and sentence end): a denial counts only against the redone calls its own clause cites or names, or, standing in a clause of its own ("; it was never redone"), the ones the clause before it named, and a kind word names the redone calls of its kind only in a clause that neither cites nor rests on a call of that kind. While these facts are shown, a rewrite of any other passage written without the code is held to what it adds: a failure of a redone call that the sentence it replaces did not claim, read narrowly (cited ids, whole-word tool names, kind words), so a limitation fact grounds no false failure in another section and a sentence of the sample's behaviour ("the comparison fails") is rewritten as before; a section 5 rewrite, the platform's own, is read by its citations alone: it cites no redone call the sentence did not cite without the call that redid it.
