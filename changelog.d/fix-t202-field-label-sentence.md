### Fixed

- **A claim sentence that opens with a field label in another casing is read (T-202).** "CLAIM: Evidence: the routine at 0x10 decodes strings." was dropped as a heading with no sentence and counted as begun and not read, because the label was matched in any casing while the field reader reads EVIDENCE and CONFIDENCE only in capitals. A block is still dropped when its sentence opens with EVIDENCE or CONFIDENCE in capitals, or with TECHNIQUE or DISSENT in any casing. A field label in any casing drops it when a ledger id follows it anywhere. Otherwise a label in another casing drops it when nothing follows the label or the first word after it, past any mark, quote, bullet or sign, is a field's value: a ledger id, a number, a technique id, NONE, TBD, "see" or a plain confidence word (very, high, medium, moderate, low, N/A, unknown). So "Evidence: (from strings) [ev_0001]", "Confidence: ledger ev_0001" and "Confidence: high because ..." are still no sentence; the sentences now read are evidence and confidence lines in another casing that cite no ledger id and whose first word is not a value. Every dropped block is still counted as unread. Prompts are unchanged.
