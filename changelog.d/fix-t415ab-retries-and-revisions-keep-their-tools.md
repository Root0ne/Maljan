### Fixed

- **A validation retry, the drops question, the whole-answer question and a built-in revision carry the agent's own tools (T-415a, T-415b).** These turns were sent with no tools, so a model asked to fix a claim could not read or check the value it was asked about; on haiku7 they were 37 of the run's 119 calls, and two of its defects (a callee never read, an absence stated of it) were written in such retries. Each of these questions now runs the agent's own tool loop over its conversation: the tool list its loop binds, in the same order and with the same bindings, guards and withholding, the loop's stall deadline, breaker and repeat guard, part of the loop before it: the questions share the time to that loop's deadline (one that cannot hold an answer at the loop's pace is not asked, and the first answer stands) and the steps it left, each spending from them, and with fewer than three steps left a question is asked in the tools-free call, its reason on the loop's record (`asked_without_tools`); their steps are not added to the agent's own, so a delegated callee stays inside its ask's steps. Each answer is checked against the ledger as it stands when it is checked, so an entry the question's own call filed may be cited. A call the model makes is filed on the ledger as any loop's is; an identical read-only call the agent's loops already made this round is answered from that entry and says it was made earlier in this analysis. Every turn is recorded under the question's own call (`validation retry`, `kept-retry drops question`, `whole answer after an unmerged retry`), its budget record rides on the loop it follows (`asked_after`), and the loop's deadline, pace and room flag stand after it. The answer is read as the tools-free answer was; a question whose loop a cap ends, or that writes no text, is asked once more in its tools-free call, worded for no tools, in the time left, so it never answers less than before; every fallback is listed with its reason in `run_summary.budget.<agent>.questions`. The retry and the whole-answer question say the tools are attached and that a claim may be fixed by citing an entry or by reading it; the drops question says an item may be read or checked before it is decided; the entries head no longer says the turn carries no tools, and the system prompt states the tools the request carries. The window measures of these turns count the tool definitions they carry. A built-in revision round runs the agent's tool loop whenever it has tools its model binds, not only when the directive names an unlisted function. A tool-carrying revision is a full loop with its own time and steps and at most one no-tool-call question per analyst per job, and its validation turn is held to what the revision's loop left. A model that cannot bind the tools, at the turn's check or when the question's loop builds its graph, is asked in the one tools-free call as before, and the agent's and the run's degradation reasons say so once. A question a cap ends does not announce that the stage ended at a cap. A retry's loop turns are counted by the spend meter as the retry they are, and the debate stage plans loop turns. The tool definitions are byte for byte the loop's, beside the loop's system prompt. Measured offline on haiku7's 37 such requests (the bodies the run logged, through today's request hook and a simulation of the provider's prefix cache that reproduces the 3.57 USD the earlier change measured): when the model calls no tool they come to 4.43 USD against 3.58, +0.85 USD, of which +0.55 the validation retries, +0.15 the drops questions and +0.15 the revisions; about 523,000 tokens of definitions are added, eight requests cross the 100,000-token price tier, and a revision's first request now writes the cache. haiku7 ran with no step or time limit, so the shared steps and time change nothing in this estimate.
