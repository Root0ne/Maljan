### Changed

- **Every mediation runs its tool loop, and the mediator may ask the reverser or the static analyst.** A round in which no analyst wrote explicit dissent used to take one call with no tools, and the judge's definition could not name another agent, so a dispute about a function nobody had read stayed open for the whole debate. Now the loop runs every round with `read_evidence` offered from its first turn, the judge's seed asks `reverser` and `static` and reads the job's sandbox report, and the model decides whether to read, look up or ask. An ask from the mediator runs through the same delegation as any other: the answer is the tool's result, the asked agent's calls are on the ledger, what its checks said is written under `judge`, and the mediator still decides. The prompt's sentence about tools is the one every agent gets; the sentence telling the mediator to use its tools only to resolve contradictions, the "No Threat Intelligence tools are needed" line and the salvage turn's "Do NOT call any more tools" are gone, the salvage turn now saying the loop has ended and the turn carries no tools. An empty answer the cap did not cut is still asked once more. In a team with a debate stage the sample is mirrored for the providers of the agents the judge may ask and the roster names them with `via: ["judge"]`; neither the job-start probe gate nor the provider readiness check refuses a job over them, since a failed ask is a failed call on the ledger. A team without a debate stage, and one that withholds every server from the judge, is unchanged. The judge's asks follow the debate stage's and the profile's tool policy, never its verdict stage's. A round that needs nothing answers on its first turn and pays only for the tool definitions it carried (about 0.002 USD a round on Claude Haiku 5.5 at a mediation prompt above 100K tokens).

### Upgrading

- A stored judge row with the earlier seed list (`knowledge` and reputation alone) reads as the new seed, so saved settings load unchanged. The settings model now accepts agent references on the judge; the reporter still takes none, and neither can be asked.
