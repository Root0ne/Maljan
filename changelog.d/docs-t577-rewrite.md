### Documentation

- **The docs are rewritten around a reader path.** Introduction, quick start, concepts (pipeline, agents and teams, evidence ledger, debate and judge, report, validation), guides, reference, operations, development and benchmark, in that order. New pages: the five concept pages, the command line, upgrades, backups and monitoring; the upgrade, backup, health and log sections moved out of Deployment and Operations onto them. The pipeline, the debate loop and the deployment topology are drawn as inline diagrams that follow the site's light and dark themes, and the examples come from a real run: a ledger entry, an analyst's claim, a mediator's contradictions, a report excerpt with its citations. Every page uses the product name and the names the code rename will give: `malspark`, `MALSPARK_*`, `github.com/Root0ne/Malspark`.
- **The README is rewritten for the announcement**, with the latest benchmark headline and a documentation table that follows the new tree.
- **The benchmark holds one run, the latest.** The ten per-run score pages are gone; their addresses lead to the benchmark page. The page states its numbers once, in a data block at its top, explains the key of facts drawn from public human reports and what counts as a fact beyond them, and lists what the run still gets wrong.
