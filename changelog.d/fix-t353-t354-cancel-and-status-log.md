### Fixed

- **A cancelled job ends the sandbox artefact fetch it has in flight at once (T-353).** The cancel already stopped the waits between tries and any further try, but a request already sent ran until its HTTP timeout, up to 120 s on CAPE, holding a worker thread. The job's cancellation now shuts down the connections of the client making the fetch, for every sandbox provider: a connection still in its TLS handshake or proxy tunnel, one waiting for the answer and one reading its body, whether it goes direct or through a configured proxy. A try that fails once the job is cancelled is said as `packet capture not fetched (cancelled)`, never as the transport error the cancel caused, and the retry policy makes no further try after it; a body that ends at the connection's close is not kept as a capture once the job is cancelled. A connection still being opened ends when its connect returns or times out.
- **A status read logs the triage pack's choice of server at debug (T-354).** `/system/status` still wrote the `triage pack: … reads server` lines of the decoder, capa and carve steps at info, because the servers a job calls were read at the default level. The level is now passed through: a status read logs them at debug, and the job start keeps info.
