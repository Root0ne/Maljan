### Fixed

- **A blocking line that only sets an analyst's claim against its own dispute entry is settled by rule, with no revision round (T-563).** haiku11's debate ran five rounds to its limit (2,577 s, 43 % of the run's wall time). Rounds 3 and 4 were opened mostly by lines such as "Claim 10 gives the name at 0.85 ... its own dispute entry holds it at 0.50", and a line about the analyst's "claim 27" against "its claim 40" was read for no analyst, so withdraw-by-rule could not act on it.
  - The platform settles such a line when its one field names one analyst, it says the contradiction is with that analyst's own words, names nobody else (by key, label or as another analyst or report) and writes no claim number it cannot settle, and each claim shares a ledger id, an address or a quoted value with the line and with a dispute entry of the same answer that names no one else and states exactly one lower figure, in the first person and as a confidence rather than a measurement, which the line states too. The claim is held at that lower figure, never raised and never withdrawn. Every settled line is told to the room, to the next mediation and to the analyst's later revisions, and recorded in the run summary (`settled_by_rule`) and the report's run appendix; a later revision that states the claim again above its held figure is recorded.
  - A settled line asks nobody to revise. When every blocking line of a mediation is settled, the debate ends as `settled_by_rule`; otherwise only the analysts the other lines name revise. The last mediation the round limit allows is settled the same way.
  - On a line whose field names one analyst, "its claim N" is read for that analyst when no other participant is named before it, and a bare "claim N" the line already names for that analyst is the same claim, not an unplaced reference.
  - Replayed over the stored debates, no line of haiku7 to haiku11 is settled. haiku11's round-3 mutex line comes closest, but each entry stating its 0.50 also quotes peers' claims and figures, so the rule leaves it to the agents. Its round-4 lines are not settled either: one also writes a claim with no lower figure of its own, the other names two analysts. Every claim reading of those mediations is unchanged but haiku11's round-4 line 1, whose "its claim 40" is now read for its analyst.
