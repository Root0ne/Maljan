### Changed

- **The composer states what a fact shows instead of refuting a named claim (T-560).** Where an analyst claim contradicts a deterministic fact, the composer's system text used to ask the model to drop the claim or "name it and refute it with the fact", which produced debate prose ("Claim 44 is refuted", "the earlier analysis said ...") that the style check then asked about again or left out of the analyst report. It now asks the model to drop the claim or state what the fact shows, in the sample's terms, without naming the claim, its number or an earlier analysis. That sentence is the only change to every composer request; the order of the request, its other text and the facts' precedence over claims are as they were. The analysts' and the debate's prompts, where refuting a claim is the job, are unchanged.
