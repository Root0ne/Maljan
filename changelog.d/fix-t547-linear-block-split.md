### Fixed

- **A long run of blank lines in an analyst's answer is split into claim blocks in one walk (T-547).** The `---` separator was tried at every newline of a whitespace run, and each try walked the rest of the run, so an answer from a model stuck repeating newlines took seconds to split: 2.3 s for 16,000 newlines and 8.3 s for 16,000 indented ones, now under 2 ms each. A separator now begins only where the whitespace before it begins, and back-to-back `---` lines are taken as one separator. The blocks read, the heading note and number each claim keeps, and the counts of claims read and begun are unchanged: over 1,000,000 generated answers (CR, CRLF, vertical tab, no-break space, line separator, chained, indented and `---x` lines, long dash runs, headings with notes) the claims read, every field included, were identical, and the stored runs replay with no difference. The claim sentence's own reading is still slow on a run of blank lines inside a claim's block; that is a separate fix.
