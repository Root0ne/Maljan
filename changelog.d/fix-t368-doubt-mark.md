### Fixed

- **A sentence that doubts a published technique by pointing back at it, or by a doubt word right before its id, is marked with the publication (T-368).** The mark added in T-337 read only a doubt that follows the id, so "(T1070.004); this is a code-path candidate and was not observed" and "(possible T1620, not remote injection)" stood unmarked beside a published technique. A clause that opens "this is a … candidate" (or "it remains unconfirmed", "this is not yet confirmed") is now bound to the id that ends the clause before it, when that id is alone in its bracket group; only code-path, static, weak or plausible may qualify the doubt, and "it was a candidate" is not read as a doubt now. A closed set of doubt words (possible, potential, probable, likely, suspected, presumed, putative, tentative, hypothetical, candidate, unconfirmed, unverified) is bound to the id it stands right before, when a closing bracket, a comma or the clause's end follows the id and three more words stand between the doubt word and the clause's start or the id before it. Neither binds in a sentence that says something is confirmed, verified, proven or corroborated. The doubt word does not bind after a negation or a word that rules the doubt out anywhere earlier in its clause ("no evidence of", "hardly", "any", "rather than", "disproves", "eliminated", "ruled it out"), after a clause that holds one and ends in a colon, or before a noun ("the possible T1071 path"). A clause pointing back does not bind to an id that a denial or a contrast stands before ("The sandbox ruled out T1564.004; it is unconfirmed"). A doubt that lasts "until" something is still marked ("remains a candidate until the sandbox runs it"), but not in a sentence that says the thing came ("until the sandbox confirmed it"). Over the stored runs the change adds exactly these two marks and removes none; the words and the stored report are never changed.
