### Fixed

- **The salvage's room is derived from the window by the context budget's own rules, and every salvage says on the run record how it was sized and what it cut (T-020).** The conversation a salvage re-sends was cut at two fifths of the window at four characters a token, a share and a second characters-per-token figure of its own beside the context budget's measured three. It is now the window less the reply reserve (never less than a quarter of the window, so a smaller `expert_max_tokens` or `judge_max_tokens` does not widen it), less a margin for what the character measure gets wrong, at the context budget's characters a token, less the tool definitions and the salvage's own question sent beside the turns. The margin is an eighth of the window where a step, time or repeat cap ended the loop (the tool answers after the server's last count were never counted), and a quarter where the server refused the conversation as over the window. From 8,192 tokens up a salvage that ended on a cap may re-send 1.875 windows' worth of characters where it was 1.6, and one after a refusal 1.5. On a window too small for the 16,000-character floor (4,096 tokens) the floor is held to the window's room before the reply, 9,216 characters: the old floor was larger than the window and could not be sent. Each salvage's row on its loop record (`run_summary.budget.<agent>.salvages`) carries `window_budget`, the sentence saying how its room was derived, and `messages_dropped` where the trim cut; the judge's reasoning salvage, which cut without a record, now writes the same row, including "not sent" with no time left or under the spend ceiling and "failed". The input room's fallback for a tokenizer that cannot load keeps its four characters a token. Over the 25 stored runs every recorded salvage fits both the old and the new room, so no stored request changes; stored runs replay byte-identical.
