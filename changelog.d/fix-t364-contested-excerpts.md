### Changed

- **A named revision is shown, of each peer, the claims the contested lines cite, not the peer's whole report (T-364).** When the mediation names who revises, each named analyst still gets its own report whole, but for each peer a line names it now gets only the claims that line cites by number. Those claims are numbered and quoted from the peer's answer in force, the one the mediator read, exactly as the mediator's summary wrote them. They are followed by any of the peer's disputes that name the analyst, under a heading such as `STATIC REPORT (claims 3, 10 quoted; the whole report is not shown):`. A peer's whole report is shown as before when a line names that peer with no claim number for it, when a number is too long or past the claims of its answer in force, or when that answer is not held. Each such case writes a sentence on the round's record and in the log. Each round's record, and `run_summary.efficiency.inputs.peer_reports`, count the excerpts and the whole reports with their character sizes, so the saving can be measured. A round that asks every analyst, the merge by claim number, the system turn and the order of the prompt's blocks are unchanged, and a whole report is headed and passed byte for byte as before.
