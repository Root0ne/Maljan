- **A debate revision is merged into the answer in force by claim, and no longer drops claims it does not mention (T-491).** In the stored haiku8 run one analyst's round-4 revision re-ran its tools, wrote a fresh answer of 10 unnumbered claims with no withdrawal, and replaced its 69 claims in force; the platform only logged "replaced 69 claim(s) with 10", and the recon commands, check-in format, encryption, persistence and self-deletion findings it had held left that analyst's answer. The revision prompt asks the analyst to revise its claims and never says whether to write the whole answer or only what changed, and the revision node took whatever it wrote as the whole answer. A revision is now merged into the answer in force: a claim it writes again replaces its predecessor; a claim it withdraws, by a `WITHDRAW CLAIM <n>: <reason>` line or by a block under the claim's number that declares the withdrawal, is removed and its reason is stated; a claim in force that a blocking line of the round's mediation named, and that the revision neither wrote again, withdrew nor argued in its disputes, is withdrawn by rule, with that line as its reason, so a challenged claim left unanswered never reaches the report as if it stood; a claim it writes under a new number, or one that restates no claim in force, is added; and every other claim in force stands unchanged, marked carried forward from that round, and, where a line that does not block named it, marked contested by that line and not answered. A line names a claim by the analyst's name, by the claim number right after a field naming one analyst, or by a short form of a field analyst's name ("Static R2 claim 3") that fits one participant alone, a word naming a participant whole naming that one; the mark names the line and its round ("L4 (round 3)"), and only the mediation right before the round is read. A revision whose every block writes a number that names a claim in force the block restates, or a number after the last one, is placed by those numbers, so claim N stays claim N; any other is placed by what it states. A revision block restates a claim when it states four fifths of the claim's content words, or half of its values that are no mere small number with at least one strong value (four characters or more, or a technique id), or a third of them with the claim's technique id; values may be pooled over blocks that each state one of the claim's values, words never are. An explicit withdrawal of many claims still shrinks the answer. A revision that restates or withdraws every claim in force stands exactly as before, so its record, the next round's request and the convergence test are unchanged; carried claims are compared as unchanged and neither fake a change nor block convergence.
