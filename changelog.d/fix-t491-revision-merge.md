### Fixed

- **A debate revision is merged into the answer in force by claim, and no longer drops claims it does not mention (T-491).** In the stored haiku8 run one analyst's round-4 revision re-ran its tools, wrote a fresh answer of 10 unnumbered claims with no withdrawal, and replaced its 69 claims in force; the platform only logged "replaced 69 claim(s) with 10", and the recon commands, check-in format, encryption, persistence and self-deletion findings it had held left that analyst's answer. The revision prompt asks the analyst to revise its claims and never says whether to write the whole answer or only what changed, and the revision node took whatever it wrote as the whole answer. A revision is now merged into the answer in force: a claim it writes again replaces its predecessor; a claim it withdraws, by a `WITHDRAW CLAIM <n>: <reason>` line or by a block under the claim's number that declares the withdrawal, is removed and its reason is stated; a claim it writes under a new number, or one that restates no claim in force, is added; and a claim in force it neither writes again nor withdraws stands unchanged, marked carried forward from that round. The mark travels with the claim to every claim summary (the peers, the mediator, the judge, the transcript) and to the evidence bundle, and the run summary and the report's negotiation section state each merge that carried claims, with their numbers, and each withdrawal with its reason. A revision whose every block writes a number that names a claim in force it shares a value with, or a number after the last one, is placed by those numbers, so claim N stays claim N; any other is placed by what it states: a claim in force is restated when the revision's claims together state at least half of its values, or four fifths of its content words (half, for a claim that states no value). An explicit withdrawal of many claims still shrinks the answer. The answer in force after a merge is rendered as a numbered summary with what the revision wrote besides its claims after it, as a merged validation retry already is. A revision that restates or withdraws every claim in force stands exactly as before, so its record, the next round's request and the convergence test are unchanged; carried claims are compared as unchanged and neither fake a change nor block convergence. The revision prompt is unchanged.
