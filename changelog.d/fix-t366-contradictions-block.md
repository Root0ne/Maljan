### Fixed

- **A blank line between two lines of the mediator's final block no longer ends it (T-366).** The reader ended the `CONTRADICTIONS:` block at the first blank line followed by a line that was not bulleted or numbered, so a mediator that wrote its lines unbulleted with a blank line between them had one line read: a paid run's third mediation listed 19 lines, 5 of them blocking, and its revision round acted on 1. A plain line that holds a field every listed line is asked for (opening with its `[analysts: ...]` field, or ending with a `[blocking ...]` / `[not blocking ...]` mark) now continues the block after a blank line as a bulleted or numbered line does; a sentence that only names a field or a mark does not. The block still ends at the agreement line, the end of the answer, and prose after a blank line, so a closing sentence is never read as a contradiction; a remark after a blank line followed by a line with those fields is read past and is no item, and after a NONE a blank line ends the block. A heading with no blank line above it is no item: inside the block it is read past, and before the block's first line it heads the lines under it when the next line is a list line or holds the fields, and otherwise ends the block, which is then unreadable and asked about once rather than read as consensus. A quoted line is never read as holding a field. Plain paragraphs without either field still end the block at a blank line, as before. Over every stored mediator answer (32), two blocks change: 1 to 19 lines and 6 to 15 lines; every other block reads as it did.
- **Each mediation's answer is kept in the run record.** The mediator's words were in no artefact, so nobody could read its final block again after a run. Each mediation the mediator answered now keeps its answer whole in `run_summary.negotiation.mediator_answers`, with its round, masked as model text in the record is; the key is absent when no answer was kept.
