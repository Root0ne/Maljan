### Changed

- **An analyst that reads code is asked once about the unread callees of the routines it describes (T-558).** Before its answer stands, the validation turn lists the callees of every routine a claim names or cites that no listing of the analyst holds, read from its own listings and the pack's function index. They are ranked by distance from where the code starts (the entry point and exports the PE header states, the threads the function index states; where none is stated, the routines a claim calls an entry, main, dispatcher or beacon routine), a further caller counting one more call so shared helpers come last. At most 12 are listed, each with its reason in addresses and counts only, and the question says how many there are. The analyst may read any of them with its tools or none; nothing is forced. The question is asked once per analyst and job and rides the turn's other questions; `run_summary.budget.<agent>.callees_question` and an answered validation row record what it listed and what the analyst read after it. In the console the list is the agent's correction line, its reads are its own tool calls, and the line ends in a new `answered` state saying what it chose. No agent or tool is recognised by name: code listings are read by the declared tool kinds, and an undeclared tool by what its name says; where the platform cannot tell, a note in the agent's conversation and `callees_question.not_run` say why, once per job. An answer with nothing unread sends what it sent before.
