\subsection{Samples, ground truth and the sandbox}\label{samples-ground-truth-and-the-sandbox}

The dynamic cohort is n=\fact{cohort-n} Windows PE samples from MalwareBazaar, stratified by year at
seed \fact{cohort-seed} with its digest recorded, of which \fact{baseline-n} produced a usable
sandbox report. Ground truth resolves a family signature to an in-repo MITRE \texttt{uses} fixture
through an alias map with a CamelCase-split fallback. It is family-level and uncurated at technique
level, so a single binary of a family need not exhibit all the techniques catalogued for that family
and absolute recall carries a structural ceiling. Absolute F1 values here are therefore not
comparable to work using per-sample expert labels; the bias is approximately constant across arms,
which makes within-study contrasts the defensible reading. The
withdrawn temporal-drift study's manifest survives as \fact{drift-n} dated samples over
\fact{drift-cohorts} cohorts and \fact{drift-families} families, metadata only.

Three properties of the sandbox bound what can be reproduced. It is CAPE 2.5 with one analysis
machine and no Linux guest, so an ELF submitted there joins the \fact{cape-backlog} tasks never
scheduled. Reports are retained for days rather than indefinitely, with
\fact{cape-report-retention-sampled} of \fact{cape-report-retention-sampled} sampled older tasks
reporting no report directory, so the local archive is the only copy and re-submitting the same
samples is not a reproduction recipe on its own. And the instance rate-limits with a throttle
response byte-identical to its auth refusal, \texttt{\{"error":\ true,\ "message":\ ""\}} in both
cases, so a harness reading that string as a capability verdict will mis-classify a throttle as a
missing permission, as ours did briefly. A new submission is scheduled in \textasciitilde1 second
and completes in \textasciitilde5.5 minutes, putting an n=\fact{cohort-n} cohort at roughly
\fact{cohort-sandbox-hours} hours of sandbox time.

Two serving details are load-bearing for anyone reproducing an arm. The local server's output cap
must be sent twice, because \texttt{ChatOpenAI(max\_tokens=N)} reaches the wire as
\texttt{max\_completion\_tokens}, which \texttt{ik\_llama.cpp} does not read, so the cap must also

both. And that server truncates silently, returning \texttt{finish\_reason:\ "stop"} at the cap and
never \texttt{"length"}, so telemetry detecting truncation by finish reason reports zero however
often the cap binds. The frontier arm ran on a free tier allowing \fact{openrouter-daily-limit}

quota mid-flight, \fact{quota-calls-landed} calls landing and \fact{quota-calls-refused} returning

\fact{baseline-n}-sample cohort is two days at that rate, and a full-pipeline arm at roughly

\subsection{Experimental design}\label{experimental-design}

Four terms are used in one sense throughout. An \emph{arm} is one condition of one experiment, a
configuration under which every input is scored, so that two arms differ in the one thing being
tested. A \emph{sample} is a real malware binary from the dated cohort, scored against its family's
MITRE \texttt{uses} set. A \emph{fixture} is one of the five synthesised inputs used by the
equal-budget arms, whose evidence is generated from its own technique list and which therefore has a
ceiling a real sample does not; samples and fixtures are different populations with different ground
truth, they are never pooled, and where both appear in one figure they appear on separate axes. The
\emph{reconciliation step} is the deterministic pass that compares the judge's bundle against the
corroboration cascade's set and restores what the judge omitted.

Arms are compared at an equal total token budget: a multi-agent arm making N calls receives B/N per
call against a single arm's one call of B, so a win cannot come from spending more. The design
follows Bertalanič and Fortuna \cite{bertalanic2026consensus}, including a stochastic-noise control
in which one analyst receives evidence irrelevant to the sample. That control establishes that the
harness can detect a difference at all: in the fixture consensus study the noise arm separated
(\fact{consensus-noise-f1} against \fact{consensus-negotiated-f1} and \fact{consensus-single-f1})
while the treatment did not, which distinguishes no effect from no sensitivity. For reasoning models
the budget must cover reasoning, because our frontier endpoint spent \fact{arm-default-reasoning} of
its output on reasoning across \fact{arm-default-n} real prompts and
\fact{reasoning-share-one-token} on a one-token answer, so capping content alone would have granted
it roughly twice the generation for the same nominal budget. Arms must also sit on the same side of
the reasoning switch: measured on two models at \fact{arm-default-n} calls each, enabling the flag
moved F1 by \fact{reasoning-flag-third-delta-2dp} on one and
\fact{reasoning-flag-vendor35b-delta-2dp} on the other, in both cases because the model spent its
entire output budget thinking and returned no answer, with \fact{arm-qwen35ba3b-hit-cap} of
\fact{arm-qwen35ba3b-n} calls hitting the cap on the second. An unmatched pair therefore does not
produce a noisy comparison; it produces a comparison of the flag.

Three further rules were adopted after each one caught something, and all three are cheap. Measure a
mechanism's firing rate before its effect: the confidence cap fires on \fact{cap-rate} of
techniques, so an ablation would describe the \fact{cap-rate-complement} where the cap never ran,
while the sink-reachability hint fires on \fact{hint-rate} of samples and an ablation of it is a
real statement about the hint. The cap's preconditions explain its rate, since it applies to three
technique families only when the sole contributing layer is static and
\fact{cap-gated-yara-only-share} of those claims are YARA-only. An ablation must therefore be
restricted to the samples where the mechanism fires, because averaging a feature over inputs it
never touched dilutes a real effect toward zero. Count how many distinct outputs the N inputs
produced, and report that count as a result column rather than as a test, because the signal exists
only in the batch while each individual response is well-formed; we report
\fact{cardinality-distinct-graphs} distinct call-graph sizes across \fact{cardinality-samples}
samples. And retain per-sample outputs for every study, since aggregates cannot be re-interrogated:
when a defect was found that might have affected a completed \fact{drift-n}-sample study, the
question could not be asked because only the summary survived. Long-lived servers accumulate state
that crosses sample boundaries, so the reverse-engineering server is restarted between samples,
which recovered \fact{switch-recovered} of \fact{switch-attempted} samples previously recorded as
unmeasurable. Two audits were added for the same reason and both caught real errors. Diffing the claim register
against the evidence log found a claim recorded as novel an hour after our own text called it a
replication, plus three ledger rows carrying superseded figures. Counter-searching each novelty
claim in an adjacent field's vocabulary demoted five claims, including the one central to this
paper, and its result is recorded whether or not it demotes anything, because a searched absence is
only credible if the looking is documented.

\subsection{Statistical analysis}\label{statistical-analysis}

Every arm sees the same samples in the same order, differences are computed per sample and then
aggregated, and 95\% bootstrap confidence intervals are taken over the paired deltas. The
observations are nested, with many claims from one sample and many samples from one family, so the
bootstrap resamples whole clusters and the p-value comes from flipping the sign of whole cluster
means rather than of rows. Two properties of that test are reported with it. With $k$ clusters the
two-sided version cannot return a p below $2/2^k$, which at $k$=\setting{5} is
\fact{fixture-signflip-floor} and puts $\alpha$=0.05 out of reach whatever the effect size; and
above \setting{20} clusters the $2^k$ assignments are sampled rather than enumerated, at
\fact{stats-iters} draws with the observed assignment counted in its own reference set, so the
smallest p that route can return is one over the draws rather than zero. Each reported p names which
route produced it. The two properties pull in opposite directions, because raising the cluster count
lifts the floor and is also what makes enumeration stop being possible; that is why the consensus
arms were re-run one sample per family. Multiplicity is controlled with Benjamini-Hochberg over two
declared families, and every null is reported with the minimum detectable effect of the design that
produced it at 80\% power, because a null without its resolution reads either as equivalence or as a
failed experiment depending on the reader.

Both choices were corrections rather than plans: every interval in an earlier draft resampled rows
rather than clusters, and an early study ranked prompt structures on a single-run parsed-claim count
whose ranking inverted when the decoding budget changed. No result in this paper rests on a single
run.
