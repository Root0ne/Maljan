\subsection{Related work}
\label{sec:related}

Language models are applied across the malware task surface: tracking malicious packages in a
registry \citep{yu2024maltracker}, detecting Android malware from application features
\citep{zhao2025apppoet} and from generated training samples \citep{rollinson2026samples}, and
summarising a sample's behaviour in prose \citep{saha2025malaware}. None produces ATT\&CK technique
identifiers from a running analysis of a Windows binary. Technique extraction itself has moved from
supervised classifiers, among them the Threat Report ATT\&CK Mapper (TRAM) \citep{mitre2023tram},
rcATT \citep{legoy2020rcatt} and TTPDrill \citep{husari2017ttpdrill}, through neural extractors such
as EXTRACTOR \citep{satvat2021extractor}, AttacKG \citep{li2022attackg} and LADDER
\citep{alam2023ladder}, to retrieval-augmented generation (RAG). TechniqueRAG
\citep{lekssays2025techniquerag} fine-tunes only the generator, because ATT\&CK defines over 550
sub-techniques against roughly 10,000 public annotated examples. A hierarchical successor cuts the
candidate space by 77.5\% \preprintcite{morbiato2026hierarchical}, and TTPrint
\preprintcite{cheng2026ttprint} keeps only candidates supported by both an evidence span and the
MITRE definition. Neither is a comparator here: both are unreviewed, and both report against
baselines that B\"uchel et al.~\cite{buchel2025sok} call ``largely incomparable'' in a survey re-evaluating more
than forty systems, which also finds traditional natural language processing (NLP) approaches
outperforming embedder-based and generative ones. Our describe-then-map design removes
technique-identifier recall from the model entirely, a domain instantiation of Infer-Retrieve-Rank
\preprintcite{doosterlinck2024incontext} stricter than TechniqueRAG \cite{lekssays2025techniquerag}, where the
model still ranks among retrieved candidates. That a retrieval backend can rank well and gate badly
is likewise not ours: Abdallah et al.~\cite{abdallah2026retrievers} treat retrieval confidence as distinct from
effectiveness and find calibration ``consistently weak across model families''.
Section~\ref{sec:assignment} measures that separation on TRAM2 \citep{mitre2023tram} and on the
independently annotated AnnoCTR corpus \citep{lange2024annoctr}. Two further results we rely on have
prior art we cite rather than compete with: that a false correction costs more than a missed one,
which is why grammatical error correction evaluates with F0.5 \citep{ng2014conll,bryant2019bea}, and
that neural text degeneration has two competing accounts, one in the likelihood objective and one in
training-data repetition \citep{welleck2019unlikelihood,li2023repetition}.

Agentic systems drive reverse-engineering backends through tool interfaces, and the models split by
role: the binary specialists are small, open-weight and fine-tuned, among them ReCopilot
\preprintcite{chen2025recopilot}, LLM4Decompile \citep{tan2024llm4decompile} and Nova
\citep{jiang2025nova}, while the models that drive the tools are frontier models. TTPDetect
\preprintcite{ttpdetect2026} is the closest system to ours, identifying techniques in stripped
binaries through retrieval-narrowed entry points and a function-level agent at 93.25\% precision and
93.81\% recall at function level and 87.37\% precision on real-world malware; we do not claim binary
evidence as an input modality, and that ground is theirs. Tool selection degrades as a catalogue
grows \preprintcite{kate2025longfunceval} and narrowing one recovers execution time
\citep{paramanayakam2025lessismore}, though neither result covers tools that overlap in what they
do. Running the model locally for confidentiality is established: REx86 \citep{lea2025rex86}
fine-tunes open-weight models for x86 reverse engineering because ``cloud-hosted, closed-weight
models pose privacy and security risks and cannot be used in closed-network facilities''.

Multi-agent debate is a standard pattern, including in security \citep{li2025phishdebate}. Two
recent results change how such claims must be read. Holding the reasoning budget constant, Tran and
Kiela find single agents match or beat multi-agent systems on multi-hop reasoning, with a crossover
appearing only at degradation 0.7 \preprintcite{tran2026single}, and
Bertalani\v{c} and Fortuna~\cite{bertalanic2026consensus} reach the same conclusion on 7B and 8B open-weight models, where
debate consumed 2.1 to 3.4$\times$ more tokens for equal or lower accuracy. Both scope their
negatives to homogeneous agents splitting one context, and both name degraded or noisy context as
the exception. That exception is our setting: a 600-import binary, a sandbox report and a network
capture are three channels rather than one context divided, and our equal-budget ablation asks
whether splitting by channel survives a control that splitting by context does not. Weighting
evidence by how far its source is trusted, and discounting sources that are not independent, is
Dempster-Shafer theory; our cascade is an instance of it with hand-chosen constants, and
Section~\ref{sec:cascade} measures how far those constants reach.

This work's methodological results belong to an established critique tradition. Arp et al.'s
\emph{Dos and Don'ts} identified ten pitfalls in machine learning for security, and its successor
\emph{Chasing Shadows} \citep{evertz2026chasing} surveys all 72 peer-reviewed LLM-security papers of
2023 and 2024, finding every one exposed to at least one of nine pitfalls and only 15.7\%
discussing any. A negative RAG result in malware analysis is already published by a different
mechanism, retrieved context diluting a signal-extraction task \citep{ng2026evaluating}. Comparing
against a trivial label-only baseline is long established \citep{metz2012baselines}, and a
systematic review by the same group found a considerable share of published multi-label results no
better than it \preprintcite{metz2015comparing}. On temporal stability the axis matters more than
the result: performance falls as evaluation moves away from the training period and scale does not
close the gap \citep{lazaridou2021mind}. That work varies the gap between training and test time;
ours fixed the model and varied the era of the input binary, and we no longer have a result on that
axis (Section~\ref{sec:limitations}).

The paper's methodological spine belongs to an area that already exists. Gunawi et al.~\cite{gunawi2008eio}
measured where an error signal is lost, finding 13\% of propagation channels drop an error code
without handling it; Yuan et al.~\cite{yuan2014simple} attributed 25\% of catastrophic failures to errors
explicitly signalled and then ignored; and Alquraan et al.~\cite{alquraan2018partition} found the majority of
network-partition failures in cloud systems to be silent. The LLM-specific instance is now
catalogued in bug taxonomies for agent orchestration frameworks \citep{xue2025agentbugs} and
inference engines \citep{liu2026inference}, the second naming both incorrect request parsing as a
cause and silent error as a symptom. Overruling a requested parameter back to a default without
saying so is itself a named misconfiguration failure mode \citep{xu2013misconfig}. Mining Model
Context Protocol (MCP) reference servers, Oyelayo et al.~\cite{oyelayo2026mcp} find data validation and type
issues to be the second most prevalent defect class at 13.97\%, which is the class our own first
defect falls into; a related constraint is that an application programming interface (API)
specification cannot express every dependency between its inputs, such dependencies being ``the
norm, rather than the exception, with 85\% of the reviewed APIs'' \citep{martinlopez2019deps}. The
detector is not new either: ``distinct inputs should produce distinct outputs'' is an ordinary
metamorphic relation \citep{li2024metamorphic}, and stress-testing an LLM judge's consistency under
input variation is established \citep{dev2026judge}. Nor is the newest of our mechanisms: our own
parameter-size series reproduced the $\rho$=0.85 at p=0.014 the nearest prior work reports
\preprintcite{ryan2026attck}, on an axis whose low-scoring row was a model disabled by a
configuration flag rather than limited by its size. That a setting can outweigh model size is
established, under output-length constraints \citep{sun2025length}, across inference-time
configurations \citep{mizrahi2024multiprompt}, and where extra test-time compute lets a smaller
model overtake a larger one \citep{snell2025testtime,hassid2024budget} or longer reasoning improves
accuracy \citep{jin2024steplength}.

Every result of ours cited above was produced on one model on one machine, except where a statement
concerns retrieval or the deterministic cascade and no model was involved at all.
